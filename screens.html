Core Screens

Three screens, three clear jobs.

Each screen is built around a single question, with the answer at the top and the details below it — so a user holding a watering can in the other hand can still use it.

ScreenQuestion it answersKey elementsDesigned state
TodayWhat do my plants need right now?Task list (max 5), snooze action, weekly progress bar3 tasks due, 7 of 10 done this week
My PlantsHow is everyone doing?Plant rows with word-based health, next-water date11 happy, 1 thirsty
Plant DetailWhat does this one plant need?Amount (“about 2 cups”), light, last / next water, care noteMonstera — water today

States, not just screens

Empty state

“No plants yet — add your first one and Fernway will build your today list.” One action, no tour.

All-done state

“All done for today. Next job is Thursday — mist the fern.” The user learns when they can stop thinking about it.

Overdue, kindly

“The pothos waited 2 extra days — a good drink today will set it right.” A fact and a fix, never a warning banner.

Add a plant in under a minute

Step 1

Name it in your words

Search common names first (“money plant”), with the Latin name shown second — never the other way round.

Step 2

Pick its room & light

Room chips plus three plain light choices: bright, medium, or low. Fernway suggests a schedule from that alone.

Step 3

Confirm the rhythm

“Water about every 7 days — adjust anytime.” The schedule is a suggestion the user owns, not a rule.
