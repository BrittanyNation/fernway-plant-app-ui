Plant care, without the guilt.

Fernway is a fictional plant care companion for people who love plants and forget the watering can. The design answers one question first — what do my plants need today? — in plain words, with no streaks, no shaming, and no red warnings for a plant that is simply thirsty.

A finishable today

Three tasks, not thirty. The list is designed to be completed, so opening the app feels good.

Words over alarms

Plants are “thirsty”, never “critical”. Every status pairs color with a plain word, for clarity and accessibility.

Snooze without shame

Every reminder can move to tomorrow in one tap, with no penalty copy and no lost streak.

Design notes

One question per screen

Today answers “what now?”, My Plants answers “how is everyone?”, and Detail answers “what does this one plant need?”

Calm color, clear jobs

Aubergine carries structure, iris marks actions, and lemon marks only what is due today — highlights stay rare so they stay meaningful.

Honest by default

Sample data is labeled as sample data, amounts are concrete (“about 2 cups”), and nothing in the concept pretends to diagnose a plant.
